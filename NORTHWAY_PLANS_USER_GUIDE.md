# Northway Plans: user guide

For Brad and Lewis. Northway Plans draws the survey floor plan and marks what was found and what
Northway recommends. You then export the plans for the Word report.

## Signing in

Open **plans.northwaypreservation.co.uk** (or the Northway Plans icon on the iPad) and sign in with
your email and password. You stay signed in on that device until you press **Sign out** in the plan
library.

## Starting a plan

In the plan library press **New Plan**:

* **Blank Plan**: draw the plan yourself.
* **Import Plan**: choose a scan exported from the iPhone or iPad. This can be an Apple RoomPlan
  `.json` or `.zip`, an OpenPlan3D project file, or a project package. Walls, doors, windows and
  fixed fittings (WC, basin, sink, bath, kitchen units) come in. Loose furniture is left out of the
  survey view.

Give it a name (for example "14 Example Street – Smith"). Customer and address are optional; the
address is printed on exports. Use **Search** to find any plan by name, customer or address. The
newest plans are at the top.

## Drawing

On the **Build** tab:

* **Draw Wall**: tap each corner. Double-tap, or tap the first corner again, to finish a room.
  Escape stops drawing.
* **Doors / Windows**: choose a type, then tap a wall.
* **Objects → Fixed fixtures**: kitchen unit, sink, hob, WC, basin, bath, shower, chimney breast,
  stairs.
* **Text Label** and **Dimension** add notes and measurements.
* **Undo / Redo** are at the top.

**Moving around:** pinch with two fingers to zoom, drag with two fingers to pan. On a laptop, scroll
to zoom and hold Space and drag to pan. A one-finger tap selects; a finger drag moves the selected
item. The iPad waits until you move or lift your finger, so a pinch never draws by accident.

## Survey Findings: what you found

Under **Survey Findings**:

* **+ Area**: pick an issue (High Moisture, Woodworm Activity, Dry Rot, …) and drag a rectangle.
  **+ Custom Finding** lets you name your own.
* **+ Pin**: tap a spot and type your own description, for example "Defective rainwater goods
  above this location".
* **+ Line**: tap points along a wall, then double-tap or press **Finish**. Describe it, for example
  "Elevated moisture readings along base of external wall".

Every finding gets a reference, **F1, F2, F3…**. Use these in the written report ("Finding F3
identifies…"). References never change: deleting F2 does not renumber F3, and F2 is never reused.

## Recommended Works: what Northway recommends

Under **Recommended Works**, press **+ Area**, **+ Pin** or **+ Line**, then:

1. **Choose the Northway Priority**:
   * **3 — Priority Work** (red): work that should be addressed promptly to prevent significant
     deterioration or further damage.
   * **2 — Recommended Work** (amber): work recommended to correct an identified defect or reduce
     the risk of deterioration.
   * **1 — Advisory Work** (yellow): lower-priority, preventative or maintenance work that would
     benefit the property.
   * **FI — Further Investigation** (grey-blue): additional inspection or opening-up is required
     before the condition or required works can be fully confirmed.
2. **Type the recommendation** in your own words. A work type is optional and only offers starting
   text.
3. **Draw or place it.**

Recommendations get **R1, R2, R3…**. The Northway Priority is Northway's own guide for homeowners; it
is not an RICS condition rating.

To change something, tap the item (or its line under **On this plan**). The panel on the right lets
you change the priority, the text, an optional quoted price (never shown on the plan), duplicate or
delete it. Changing the priority keeps the same R number and shape.

Older plans saved before priorities existed show **Priority required** (grey **?**). Choose a priority
for each one before the final export.

## Saving

* Press **Save** (or Ctrl/Cmd+S). The top bar says **Saving…** and then **Saved** only once the plan
  is safely stored.
* **Unsaved changes** means you have edits that are not stored yet.
* **Save failed** or **Offline — not saved** means the plan could not be stored. Your changes stay on
  the device. Press Save again when the connection is back.
* If the iPad app closes, Safari reloads, or your sign-in ends, your unsaved changes are kept on that
  device. When you reopen the plan you will see **Unsaved changes from this device were found**,
  with **Restore my changes** and **Discard them**.
* Leaving a plan with unsaved changes asks first.

## Exporting for the report

**Export → Survey Plan (PNG / PDF)…**:

1. Choose the **floor** (if there is more than one) and set the **floor name** and **survey date**.
   These are saved with the plan.
2. Choose the plan:
   * **Survey Findings Plan**: what was identified (issue colours, F references).
   * **Recommended Works Plan**: what Northway recommends, with the priority colours, R references
     and the Northway Priority Guide.
   * **Combined Plan** (optional): both, for simple surveys.
3. **Download PNG (300 dpi)** for Word. The "Word report" size drops in at full page width with
   readable text; just insert the picture. **Download PDF (A4)** gives a full A4 page.

Export each floor separately. File names use the address and floor, for example
`14-Example-Street-Ground-Floor-Survey-Findings.png`.

## iPad

* **Install on the Home Screen:** open the site in **Safari** → the **Share** button → **Add to Home
  Screen** → **Add**. Open Northway Plans from the icon. It runs full-screen like an app. Sign in once
  inside the app.
* Use the iPad in **landscape** for editing; portrait works, with a reminder you can dismiss.
* Apple Pencil works like a finger.
* In cellars and roof spaces without signal, keep working. Save when you are back in signal.
